About the academy

A calm, personal way to learn the Qur'an

Dar-ul-Quran Online Academy was founded by Samra to make authentic, one-on-one Qur'an education accessible to students everywhere — from complete beginners learning their first letters to those refining their Tajweed.

Every class is taught live, one student at a time, in short 25-minute sessions designed to hold focus, build consistency, and make steady progress without overwhelming a busy schedule.

Whether you're a child starting with Qaida, an adult returning to the Qur'an, or someone wanting to perfect their Salah, you'll be guided with patience, structure, and care.

What we teach

Courses offered

A clear path from first letters to confident, correct recitation — chosen to match where each student is starting from.

01

Madani Qaida

The foundation stage — Arabic letters, correct pronunciation and joining rules, taught step by step for absolute beginners of any age.

02

Qur'an Recitation

Nazra reading with attention to fluency and accuracy, alongside memorisation (Hifz) support for students who wish to continue further.

03

Qur'ani Verses

Short surahs and selected verses learned with correct pronunciation and meaning, building a growing collection of Qur'an by heart.

04

Tajweed

The rules of correct recitation — articulation points, elongation and pronunciation — practised with live correction, letter by letter.

05

Wudu

The correct steps of ablution taught in full — order, sunnah acts, and the accompanying duas — so students can prepare for prayer with confidence.

06

Salah

Daily prayers taught in full — positions, recitations, and required duas — so students can pray with understanding and confidence.
